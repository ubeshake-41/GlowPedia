# Glowpedia User Types & Permissions

This document clearly defines the three user types and their distinct capabilities.

---

## 🌐 **GUEST (No Account)**

**Philosophy:** Full educational access, no barriers to learning

### What Guests Can Do:
✅ View all product wiki pages  
✅ View all comparison pages  
✅ Browse Discover More section  
✅ Browse all categories  
✅ Take 2 free quizzes  
✅ View quiz results (current session only)  
✅ View 3 starter routines  
✅ Use skin type selector  
✅ Search products  

### What Guests Cannot Do:
❌ Save quiz results  
❌ Bookmark/favorite products  
❌ Save routines  
❌ Track any progress  
❌ Access account dashboard  

### Guest Experience:
- **No login required** for any content
- **Soft prompts** to create account after delivering value
- **Never blocked** from viewing educational content
- Results/selections lost when browser closes

---

## 🆓 **FREE ACCOUNT**

**Philosophy:** Personal skincare hub - save, organize, and track basics

### Everything Guests Can Do, PLUS:

#### **Save & Organize:**
✅ **Save quiz results** - Store skin type and routine recommendations  
✅ **Bookmark products** - Create personal wishlist of favorites  
✅ **Save routines** - Keep starter routines customized for your skin type  

#### **Basic Tracking:**
✅ **Quiz history** - See when you took quizzes, compare results over time  
✅ **Saved items dashboard** - View all bookmarks and saved routines in one place  
✅ **Journey start date** - Track when you began your skincare journey  

### What Free Accounts Cannot Do:
❌ Check off routine steps daily  
❌ Take advanced Pro quizzes  
❌ Create custom routines  
❌ Access Glow AI  
❌ Upload progress photos  
❌ View detailed analytics  
❌ Get AI-powered routine adjustments  

### Free Account Experience:
- **Quick sign-up** with email only
- **No credit card** required
- **Persistent data** across devices
- **Clear upgrade prompts** for Pro features
- **Value delivered** before asking for upgrade

---

## 👑 **PRO ACCOUNT** (Paid)

**Philosophy:** Advanced personalization, tracking, and AI guidance

### Everything Free Accounts Can Do, PLUS:

#### **Advanced Quizzes:**
✅ **Pro quizzes** - Beauty Style Quiz, Routine Optimizer, Ingredient Match  
✅ **Unlimited retakes** with full history  

#### **Routine Tracking:**
✅ **Daily tracker** - Check off steps as you complete them  
✅ **Completion streaks** - See your consistency over time  
✅ **Routine history** - View patterns and habits  
✅ **Custom routines** - Create unlimited personalized routines  
✅ **Edit & adjust** - Modify routines based on what works  

#### **AI & Advanced Features:**
✅ **Glow AI** - Personal AI skincare assistant  
✅ **AI-powered adjustments** - Get routine suggestions based on progress  
✅ **Progress photos** - Upload and track skin improvements  
✅ **Detailed analytics** - Charts and insights on your skincare journey  
✅ **Routine reminders** - Push notifications to stay consistent  

### Pro Account Experience:
- **Paid subscription** (monthly or annual)
- **Full personalization** with AI
- **Advanced tracking** and analytics
- **Priority support**
- **Early access** to new features

---

## 🎯 **Clear Distinctions**

### **Guest → Free Account**
**Trigger:** "Want to save this?"  
**Value:** Keep your stuff, access from anywhere  
**Action:** Create free account (email only)

### **Free Account → Pro**
**Trigger:** "Want to track daily progress?"  
**Value:** Advanced tracking, AI guidance, custom routines  
**Action:** Upgrade to Pro (payment required)

## 🔄 **Upgrade Prompts**

### **Guest → Free (Soft, Value-First)**
- After quiz: "Save your results?"
- After viewing routine: "Save this routine?"
- On product page: "Bookmark this product?"
- **Never blocks content**

### **Free → Pro (Clear Value)**
- On routine page: "Track daily completion?"
- On quiz hub: "Unlock advanced quizzes?"
- On dashboard: "Get AI-powered guidance?"
- **Shows what you're missing**

## 💡 **Implementation Notes**

### **Database Requirements:**
- **Free:** User profile, saved items (quizzes, routines, bookmarks)
- **Pro:** Everything in Free + daily tracking data, custom routines, photos, AI interactions

### **UI Indicators:**
- **Guest:** No account indicator
- **Free:** "Free" badge in account menu
- **Pro:** "👑 Pro" badge in account menu

### **Upgrade Flow:**
- **Guest → Free:** Email sign-up modal (no payment)
- **Free → Pro:** Payment modal with benefits list
